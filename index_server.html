How to play

Teams of robots take turns. On your turn: move, aim, pick a weapon and fire. Knock enemy robots into the sea, blast their health to zero, and survive. Wind pushes rockets and plasma. Walk over supply crates for health or ammo.

On a touch screen use the cross-shaped pad on the left (walk / aim) and the buttons on the right: hold FIRE to charge, release to shoot. Swipe the screen to look around. The game plays in landscape.
